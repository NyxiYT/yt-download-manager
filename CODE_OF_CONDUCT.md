# Code of Conduct

This project should be a friendly place for everyone, whatever their experience, background or identity.

## Please do

- Be kind and patient, especially with people who are new.
- Assume good intent. Ask before you judge.
- Give feedback about the work, not the person.
- Accept that others may decide differently than you would.

## Please don't

- Insult, harass or threaten anyone.
- Post sexual, violent or hateful content.
- Share other people's private information.
- Spam, or keep pushing a topic after you were asked to stop.

## What happens

Comments, issues, discussions or pull requests that break these rules may be edited, hidden or deleted.
People who keep breaking them may be blocked from the project.

## Reporting

If someone breaks these rules, please report it privately through GitHub's
[report content](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam)
feature, or contact the maintainer through the [security advisory form](https://github.com/NyxiYT/yt-download-manager/security/advisories/new)
if the matter is sensitive. Reports are kept confidential.

This code applies everywhere in the project: issues, discussions, pull requests and reviews.
